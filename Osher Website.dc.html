About Osher

Where little joys inspire big dreams.

Osher began with a simple observation: children spend their days becoming someone else. A cape at breakfast, a crown by afternoon, a cricketer before bed. Those years pass quickly, and photographs only capture part of them.

We make something you can hold. Every figurine starts from your child's own photographs, is sculpted into a 3D model by our designers, printed in fine detail, and then painted by hand — one brush, one figure at a time.

Every childhood has a chapter like this one. We make sure it doesn't disappear.
